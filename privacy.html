PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Personal Data is Collected

We collect the following personal information to provide our services:

  • Discord username
  • League of Legends in-game name (IGN)
  • Order details (rank, wins, queue type)
  • IP address (for rate limiting purposes)

2. How Data is Used

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your boost orders
  • To send order notifications
  • To communicate with you via Discord

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Vercel KV (Upstash Redis) and is encrypted in transit. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data is protected against unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention

Order records are retained for a period of 90 days. After this period, they are automatically deleted from our systems.

5. Third-Party Services

We utilize the following third-party services, which may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics: Traffic analytics
  • Tawk.to: Live chat widget
  • Discord: Communication platform
  • Vercel: Hosting and speed insights

6. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our site, including:

  • Google Analytics cookies for traffic analysis
  • Tawk.to session cookies for live chat functionality

7. Data Selling

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third parties under any circumstances.

8. Data Requests & Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To make a request, please contact stain.hs on Discord.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the "Last updated" date visible.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via Discord by messaging the username stain.hs.
