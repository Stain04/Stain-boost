<h3>Why These Runes?</h3>
<p><strong>Lethal Tempo</strong> stacks attack speed quickly and synergizes with Yi's E (true damage procs) and R. <strong>Triumph</strong> heals on kills keeping you alive through chained teamfight resets. <strong>Legend: Alacrity</strong> adds more attack speed that compounds with your items. <strong>Coup de Grace</strong> deals 8% more damage to enemies below 40% HP — perfect for finishing off targets and securing kills in teamfights. <strong>Cash Back</strong> gives you a 10% discount on your first completed item — effectively free gold that helps you hit your Kraken Slayer spike faster. <strong>Triple Tonic</strong> triples the effect of your first Smite and potions, keeping you healthy through early clears without burning extra gold on consumables.</p>

<h3>Why This Build?</h3>
<p><strong>Kraken Slayer</strong> is your first major item — the true damage every third hit shreds tanks and bruisers. <strong>Rageblade</strong> second immediately doubles your on-hit effects and attack speed, letting Kraken Slayer proc constantly. <strong>Wit's End</strong> third adds magic damage on-hit and magic resist, making you durable against AP while compounding everything Rageblade already doubled. <strong>Death's Dance</strong> and <strong>Guardian Angel</strong> round out your survivability so you can actually survive long enough to deal your damage.</p>
<p><strong>Blade of the Ruined King</strong> is great against high-HP tanks. <strong>Mercurial Scimitar</strong> if the enemy has a suppression or hard CC that stops your R. <strong>Sterak's Gage</strong> when you're getting burst before you can fight back.</p>
