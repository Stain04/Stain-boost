<p>"How much does an ELO boost cost?" depends on three things: <strong>how far you want to climb</strong>, <strong>how high up the ladder you start</strong>, and <strong>whether the booster plays on your account or with you</strong>. This guide breaks down real prices for every tier, explains what moves the price up or down, and shows how to get the best value from a boost.</p>
<p>All prices below are StainBoost's prices as of October 2026, in US dollars. The <a href="/pricing">pricing page</a> always shows the exact, current price for your order before you pay.</p>

<h2 id="two-types">Two Ways to Buy a Boost</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rank boost (division boost):</strong> you pick your current rank and your target rank, and pay per division climbed. Best when you have a specific goal, like reaching Gold or Platinum.</li>
<li><strong>Net wins (win boost):</strong> you pay per net win in your current rank. Best for a smaller push, or when you're close to a promotion. <a href="/blog/what-is-a-win-boost">What is a win boost?</a> explains the difference in detail.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-changes">What Makes a Boost Cost More or Less</h2>

<h3>Solo or Duo</h3>
<p>Solo (the booster plays on your account) is the cheaper option. Duo (you play together, on your own account) costs more because it takes two players and you never share your login. <a href="/blog/duo-boost-vs-solo-boost">Duo vs solo</a> compares them in full.</p>

<h3>Your LP Gains Don't Change the Price</h3>
<p>Some services charge more when your account gains only a few LP per win, because each division takes more games. StainBoost doesn't: <strong>a division costs the same whatever your LP gains are</strong>. If your gains are low, <a href="/blog/why-lp-gains-are-low">here's why it happens</a>.</p>

<h3>LP You Already Have</h3>
<p>If you're partway through your current division, you only pay for the part that's left: the first division is discounted by the LP you already have.</p>

<h3>Optional Extras</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Priority start</strong> (+18%): your order goes to the front of the queue.</li>
<li><strong>Bonus win</strong> ($1.45–$6.65 by tier, rank boost only): one extra net win after you reach your target, so you don't land on 0 LP.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="save">Ways to Pay Less</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Referral code:</strong> a friend's code takes 20% off your first order.</li>
<li><strong>Net wins for short climbs:</strong> if you're near a promotion, a few wins can be cheaper than a full division boost.</li>
<li><strong>Pay for 5, get 6:</strong> ordering wins in multiples of 5 gets you the free ones.</li>
<li><strong>Solo instead of duo</strong>, if you're comfortable with the booster playing on your account.</li>
</ul>
