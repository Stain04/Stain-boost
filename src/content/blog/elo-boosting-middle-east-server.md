<h2 id="me-server-unique">What Makes the ME Server Unique</h2>
<p>The Middle East server — officially launched by Riot to serve players across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Jordan, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Iraq, and surrounding countries — has a distinct ranked environment that sets it apart from EUW or NA.</p>
<h3>Highly Aggressive Playstyle</h3>
<p>ME server lobbies are known for their fast, skirmish-heavy gameplay. Players prioritise early kills and lane dominance over scaling, which makes the average game shorter and more chaotic than on Western servers. For a booster, this means champion selection is critical — champions that can snowball early and close out games efficiently perform significantly better here than late-game scalers.</p>
<h3>Smaller Active Player Pool</h3>
<p>Compared to EUW or EUNE, the ME server has a smaller ranked player base. In higher elo, this means you frequently face the same players — everyone in Diamond and above knows each other. In lower elo, it means matchmaking occasionally widens its MMR window during off-peak hours, which can produce uneven games. A booster who plays on ME server daily understands these patterns and times sessions accordingly.</p>
<h3>Regional Latency Expectations</h3>
<p>A booster connecting from outside the region will have noticeably higher ping, which directly impacts mechanical performance on fast champions. A local booster connecting from within the Middle East plays at native latency — typically 15–40ms — which means full mechanical output on every champion. This is not a minor detail: at 120ms ping, certain combos and reaction-based plays become physically impossible.</p>
