<ul>
<li><strong>Offline mode</strong> before every login, so your friends list never sees someone else playing.</li>
<li><strong>A VPN matched to your country</strong>, so your account doesn't log in from an unusual location.</li>
<li><strong>Hand-played games only</strong> — no bots, no scripts.</li>
<li><strong>One booster</strong>: your login is used by one person and never stored.</li>
</ul>
